Place the following images in this folder:

  imran-khan.jpg   — Photo of Imran Khan (any good cricket photo)
  wc1992.jpg       — World Cup 1992 / Pakistan World Cup celebration photo

Images are used as barely-visible watermarks (opacity ~5%) across the app.
Any resolution works — larger is better quality at full-page scale.

If images are missing, the watermark layer is silently skipped (onerror fallback).
